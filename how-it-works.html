❓ FAQ

Common Questions

Is Cardify free to use?

Yes — 100% free, forever. No account required, no hidden fees, no watermarks on your card.

Is my data stored anywhere?

No. Everything runs locally in your browser. Your details never leave your device — no servers, no databases.

Can I upload my own photo?

Yes. Click the photo upload area to choose any image from your device. It renders immediately on the card with a gold border.

Why is the ID card live?

The card is built with React's controlled form pattern — every input is bound to state, so the card re-renders on every keystroke.

Can I customise the college name?

Absolutely. There's a dedicated field for your college or university name — it appears prominently in the card header.

Does it work on mobile?

Yes. The layout is fully responsive. On smaller screens the form stacks above the preview for easy use on phones and tablets.
